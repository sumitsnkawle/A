Axion Regulus is an AI-powered compliance intelligence platform. It reads your regulatory documents and policies, converts them into structured rules, and checks business content against them, with findings, evidence and recommended actions your reviewers can act on.

How it works

Key outcomes

Faster review turnaround

First-level checks are automated, so reviewers start from a structured result, not a 90-page circular.

Consistent risk scoring

Every submission is scored against the same rules (high / medium / low risk), whoever is on shift.

Risks caught before publishing

Potential issues surface before content reaches customers or regulators.

An audit-ready evidence trail

Each finding links to the rule that triggered it, the supporting evidence and the reviewer's decision.
