Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what information Axion Consultancy Services ("Axion", "we") collects through this website, axionast.com, and how we use it. If you have any questions, email info@axionast.com.

Who we are

Axion Consultancy Services is a technology company based in the United Arab Emirates and the developer of Axion Regulus. We are responsible for the information you send us through this website.

Information you give us

When you use the contact form, we receive the details you enter: your name, work email address, company, industry, regulatory framework or country, the type of inquiry, and any message you write. These fields are optional except your name and email address.

We use this information only to respond to your inquiry, for example to arrange a demo or to discuss a partnership or investment, and to keep a record of our correspondence with you. We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising.

Services that handle your information

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) receives the contact form and forwards it to us by email. Its own privacy policy applies to how it processes your submission.
  • Our email provider stores the messages we receive and send.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) hosts this website. Like any web host, it may record technical information such as your IP address when you load a page, for security and to operate the service.
  • Google Fonts (Google) and cdnjs (Cloudflare) supply the fonts and an animation library used by the site. Your browser connects to them to download these files, which shares your IP address and browser details with them.

Cookies and similar technologies

We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. The site saves one setting in your browser's local storage: the language you choose, so that we can show you that language on your next visit. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

The Mira assistant on the website answers questions inside your browser. What you type into it is not sent to us or stored.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiry emails for as long as we need them to handle your inquiry and any business relationship that follows, and longer only where the law requires it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, data-protection laws such as the UAE Personal Data Protection Law or the EU General Data Protection Regulation may give you the right to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to object to how we use it. To make a request, email info@axionast.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and update the date above.
