A SHORT STORY ABOUT THE VISION

From a 90-page regulation to a clear answer

It is late on a Thursday. A compliance officer has a new campaign to approve by morning, and a 90-page regulatory circular open in another tab. The rules are all there — somewhere between clause 4.2 and an appendix — but turning them into a confident yes or no means hours of reading, cross-checking and second-guessing.

Anastasiia Bolotna founded Axion around a simple idea: that moment should not depend on who happens to be on shift, or how tired they are. Regulations should be read once, carefully, and turned into structured rules that any team can apply the same way, every time.

That idea became Axion Regulus. The officer uploads the circular; Regulus structures it into clear requirements. They paste in the campaign; Regulus highlights what may be at risk, explains why, and suggests what to change. The final decision stays with the person — now made in minutes, with every step on record.

“Compliance shouldn’t be the reason good ideas slow down. Our vision is a world where compliance intelligence is built into every business decision — with people always in control.”

— Anastasiia Bolotna, Founder & General Manager

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Axion Regulus? +

Axion Regulus is an AI-powered claims compliance platform for food, supplement and cosmetics brands. It converts claim regulations and your own guidelines into structured rules, and uses them to check labels, packaging, product pages and ads for potential compliance risks, with findings and recommended actions.

How does Axion Regulus work? +

Organizations provide the relevant regulatory documents or policy requirements. Axion Regulus uses AI to identify and structure applicable requirements into compliance rules. Business content can then be submitted for validation against those rules, producing compliance findings, risk information and suggested actions.

Does Axion Regulus make the final compliance decision? +

No. Axion Regulus is designed to assist compliance teams with analysis and risk identification. Human reviewers remain responsible for reviewing findings and making the final business or compliance decision. AI assists. Humans decide.

What types of content can be checked? +

Depending on the configured regulations, Axion Regulus can check claims on labels and packaging, product pages and marketplace listings, advertising, social media and influencer content, and brochures or sales materials.

Can we use our own regulatory documents? +

Yes. Axion Regulus is designed to work with organization-specific regulatory documents, policies and compliance requirements. These requirements can be processed into structured rules that can then be used when validating relevant business content.

What do we receive after a compliance check? +

The platform can provide compliance analysis including identified findings, potential risks, applicable rule information, recommendations and supporting compliance evidence, depending on the configured workflow and available data.

Can Axion Regulus guarantee that content is compliant? +

No. Axion Regulus is a compliance intelligence and decision-support platform. Its analysis is intended to help teams identify potential issues and review content against configured requirements. Final compliance decisions should be made by the organization's appropriate human reviewers.

How is AI used in Axion Regulus? +

AI is used to assist with understanding regulatory content, structuring requirements, identifying potential compliance risks and analyzing submitted business content. Human oversight remains part of the workflow so that organizations can review findings before taking action.

How is customer data protected? +

Axion Regulus uses access controls, role-based permissions and controlled document processing to support the protection of business and regulatory information. Compliance activity and relevant workflow information can also be maintained for review and audit purposes where supported by the configured system.

Is Axion Regulus legal advice? +

No. Axion Regulus is a compliance intelligence and decision-support platform. It helps organizations structure regulatory requirements, evaluate business content and identify potential compliance risks. It does not replace qualified legal, regulatory or compliance professionals.

Can Axion Regulus support different regulatory frameworks? +

Yes. The platform is designed to work with organization-specific regulatory documents and compliance requirements. The applicable framework and source documents can be configured according to the organization's compliance needs.

How can we evaluate Axion Regulus? +

Request a live demonstration and bring a regulatory document, policy requirement or suitable business-content example. The Axion team can demonstrate how requirements can be transformed into structured compliance checks and how potential risks are identified.
